### 2. Root Causes of the 3 False Positives (`KEPT` but labeled `n`)
All three filter errors stem from **missing negative rules** rather than mis-scored positive keywords:
1. **`Data Scientist` (Score 7: `R2;T1;T2;T3;T4;T5`) and `Data Analyst` (Score 6: `R4;T1;T2;T3`):** Both postings matched the exact target job titles (`R2`, `R4`) and core technical stack (`Python`, `SQL`, `pipelines`, `machine learning`, `Tableau`), **but were non-US locations and/or experienced roles** lacking an explicit seniority title token (`Senior`/`Staff`/`Lead`). Because `FILTER.md v1` has no **location exclusion rule** (e.g., rejecting non-US offices when `Remote` is not US-eligible) and only catches seniority via title keywords (`X1`), experienced international `Data Scientist` / `Data Analyst` postings easily clear the threshold of `5`.
2. **`GTM Strategy/Operations Associate` (Score 6: `R5;T1;T2;T5`):** Fired on `R5` (`Associate` in title) combined with peripheral analytics keywords (`Python`, `SQL`, `dashboards`) in the preferred qualifications. `Associate` is an ambiguous seniority/function token that frequently appears in **Go-To-Market (GTM), Strategy, Business Operations, and Finance** titles (`GTM Strategy/Operations Associate`, `Associate, Strategic Finance & Investor Relations`, `Infrastructure Business Operations Associate`) rather than technical data/AI IC roles.

### 3. False-Friend Analysis (Section 4)
Reviewing the 69 candidate rule matches reveals four recurring false-friend patterns in `FILTER.md v1`:
- **`T3` (`pipeline` / `pipelines`) — Sales vs. Data Engineering collision:** `T3` fired repeatedly on B2B sales, marketing, and solutions roles where `"pipeline"` refers to **sales/revenue pipeline** (`Senior Majors Account Executive`, `Business Development Representative`, `Senior Account Executive`, `Principal Solutions Architect`, `Head of Enterprise Sales`, `Senior Partner Marketing Manager`, `Strategic Account Executive`, `GTM Strategy & Operations Senior Associate`). It also fired on **software/telemetry infrastructure** (`Software Engineer - Edge`, `Technical Escalations Engineer II`, `Software Engineer, New Grad - Query Engines`) and **customer deployment** (`Deployment Strategist`).
- **`T5` (`forecasting`) — Financial/Sales vs. Time-Series ML collision:** `T5` matched **sales quota forecasting** (`Senior Majors Account Executive`, `Customer Experience Manager`, `Strategic Account Executive`) and **corporate FP&A / guidance forecasting** (`Associate, Strategic Finance & Investor Relations`, `Finance Business Partner, G&A`).
- **`R5` (`Associate` / `New Grad`) — Overly broad title trigger:** `Associate` fired on business/finance operations roles (`GTM Strategy/Operations Associate`, `Associate, Strategic Finance`, `Infrastructure Business Operations Associate`), while `New Grad` fired on adjacent software/consulting tracks (`Forward Deployed Software Engineer, New Grad`, `Deployment Strategist, New Grad`, `Software Engineer, New Grad - Query Engines`) that are outside Jordan Lee's four target data/AI roles.
- **`T1`–`T5` on senior/research/adjacent technical roles:** Core technical tokens (`Python`, `SQL`, `pipelines`, `machine learning`, `PyTorch`, `time series`, `Tableau`) legitimately appeared in non-target roles such as `AI Research Scientist (DAIR)`, `AI Research Engineer (DAIR)` (research/PhD-level track), `Senior Financial Systems Analyst`, `Developer Advocate`, and `Manager I, Applied Science`. When a posting lacks a title exclusion token (`X1`/`X2`), these technical tokens push non-target roles over the keep threshold.

### 4. Recommended Updates for `FILTER.md` v2
1. **Add a strict Location Gate (`X3`):** Reject postings located outside the United States unless explicitly marked `Remote` (US-eligible).
2. **Add Functional / Domain Title Exclusions (`X4`):** Explicitly exclude non-target functions that reuse analytics keywords, including `GTM`, `Strategy & Operations` / `Business Operations`, `Strategic Finance`, `Investor Relations`, `Financial Systems`, `Forward Deployed`, `Deployment Strategist`, `Developer Advocate`, `Solutions Architect`, and generic `Software Engineer` titles lacking a Data/ML/AI qualifier.
3. **Restrict `R5` (`Associate` / `New Grad`):** Require `R5` to co-occur with a target data/analytics/AI title token (`R1`–`R4`) rather than awarding standalone title points to generic `Associate` or `New Grad` titles.
4. **Disambiguate `T3` (`pipeline`) and `T5` (`forecasting`):** Require data-engineering context for `pipeline` (e.g., `data pipeline`, `ETL`, `ELT`, `ingestion`, `Airflow`, `dbt`, `Kafka`, `Spark`) and exclude sales/finance context (`sales pipeline`, `quota`, `attainment`, `revenue`, `guidance`, `FP&A`).
5. **Check Experience Requirements in Description (`X5`):** Reject roles requiring `3+` or `5+` years of industry experience or PhD-only research requirements (`AI Research Scientist` / `DAIR`) even when `Senior`/`Manager` is absent from the title.
